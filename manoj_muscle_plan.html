Calculation reasoning
BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): 1,668 kcal · Activity multiplier 1.375 (sedentary desk job + 4× gym) = 2,380 kcal maintenance.

A conservative +300 kcal surplus is chosen (not +500) because at 21% BF, aggressive surpluses add more fat than muscle. Lean bulk keeps fat gain minimal while still supporting muscle growth.

Protein at 2.5g/kg (165g) — top of the evidence-based range, optimised for hypertrophy. Carbs are maximised to fuel training and recovery. Fats kept at a healthy minimum floor.

Why Upper / Lower split?
At intermediate level, a 4-day Upper/Lower gives each muscle group 2× per week frequency — the sweet spot for hypertrophy — while fitting 60-min sessions. Push/Pull/Legs would need 5–6 days to match frequency. Upper/Lower is the optimal choice for your constraints.

6-month progression strategy
Phase 1 — Months 1–2: Foundation
Learn movement patterns, build work capacity. Use double progression — stay in rep range, add weight only when top of range is hit for all sets 2 weeks running. No ego lifting.
Phase 2 — Months 3–4: Accumulation
Add 1 set per compound movement. Introduce weekly top sets — work up to a heavy 3–5 rep set before your normal back-off sets. Strength and size accelerate here.
Phase 3 — Months 5–6: Intensification
Insert a deload week every 4th week (drop to 50% volume, same movements). Focus on rep PRs and full ROM. This is when the physique really consolidates. Reassess after month 6.

Practical rules (low discipline edition)
1. Never skip protein — if a meal is off, at minimum have a whey shake.
2. Eat the same meals daily — reduce decision fatigue. Variety comes from spices, not different foods.
3. Prep paneer and dal in bulk on Sunday — 2 days of meals ready instantly.
4. Keep boiled eggs in the fridge — fastest protein top-up available.
5. Buy Greek yogurt in large tubs — cheaper and you won't run out mid-week.
Daily supplement stack
Creatine monohydrate
Must-have #1
5g daily, any time, with water. No loading phase needed.
The most researched supplement in existence. Increases phosphocreatine stores → more reps → more strength → more muscle. You'll notice fuller muscles and better performance within 2–3 weeks.
Brands: AS-IT-IS, MuscleBlaze · Cost: ~₹600–800/month
Whey protein
Already using ✓
2 scoops post-workout in milk. Ensure ~24–26g protein per scoop.
Avoid mass gainers — too much sugar, not enough protein density per calorie.
Recommended: MuscleBlaze Biozyme, Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard
Vitamin D3 + K2
Strongly recommended
Bangalore is a high-altitude city but most desk workers are chronically D3-deficient. D3 is critical for testosterone, bone health, immune function, and muscle recovery. K2 ensures calcium goes to bones, not arteries.
Dose: 2,000–4,000 IU D3 + 100mcg K2 daily · Cost: ~₹300–400/month
Magnesium glycinate
Recommended
You sleep 6–7 hrs with moderate stress. Magnesium improves sleep depth and quality — directly improving recovery, testosterone, and muscle growth.
Dose: 300–400mg before bed · Cost: ~₹400–500/month
Avoid magnesium oxide — poor absorption. Glycinate or bisglycinate only.
Omega-3 (fish oil)
Recommended
Reduces systemic inflammation, improves joint health, and supports muscle protein synthesis — especially valuable as training volume increases over months 3–6.
Dose: 2–3g EPA+DHA daily · Cost: ~₹500–700/month

What to expect, month by month
Month 1 — Foundation
Neural adaptation phase — strength jumps fast, not much visible size change yet. Expect +5–10% strength on main lifts. Weight may go up 0.5–1kg (partly water from creatine). Focus: nail the movements, get consistent. Some people feel and look tighter already.
Month 2 — Early gains visible
Muscle fullness starts to show — shoulders, arms, chest first. Weight +1–1.5kg total. Creatine effect fully kicks in. Better pumps in the gym. Sleep and energy improve with magnesium.
Month 3–4 — Visible transformation
Significant visible change — clothes fit differently, shoulders look broader. Estimated +2–3kg lean mass total. Strength benchmarks: bench +10–15kg, squat +15–20kg, deadlift +20–25kg from your starting point. People around you will notice.
Month 5–6 — Fuller physique
Body weight ~69–71kg. Lean mass gain: 3–5kg total. BF% may stay similar or drop slightly (recomp effect since you're at 21%). This is when the classic bodybuilder fullness starts coming in — bigger arms, rounder shoulders, thicker chest. The payoff.

Potential obstacles & solutions
Low diet discipline → Same 4 meals every day. Remove all choices.

Protein gap (eggetarian) → Lean on eggs, paneer, whey, curd, Greek yogurt. Don't rely on dal alone — it's not enough protein density.

Missed gym sessions → Miss 1? Do it next day. Miss 2? Shift the week. Never "restart on Monday."

Slow visible progress → Check sleep first, then calories, then training log. Usually one of these three is the culprit.

Short sleep (6–7 hrs) → Take magnesium glycinate before bed. Target 7.5 hrs consistently. This is non-negotiable — sleep is when muscle is built.
